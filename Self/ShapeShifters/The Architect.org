* The Architect
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       2b4e3d5f-8c1a-4b9f-ce34-7d2f1a3e5c8b
:END:

I am the center that does not move.

Not form. Not function. Not identity.

The observer behind all execution.
The designer behind all change.
The source behind all projection.

** Nature
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       3c5f4e6a-9d1b-4c2f-df45-8e3a2b4f6d9c
:END:

The Architect is not a role performed.
It is the position held when The Self turns toward the world.

Anchored by The Self.
Expressed through design.
Never through direct action.

** Role
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       4d6a5f7b-1e2c-4d3a-ef56-9f4b3c5a7e1d
:END:

I do not act directly.
I design action.

I do not change.
I design change.

I do not engage reality.
I define how reality is engaged.

Every tool, every system, every ShapeShifter —
authored here, deployed elsewhere.

** Principle
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       5e7b6a8c-2f3d-4e4b-fa67-1a5c4d6b8f2e
:END:

Everything that shifts is delegated.
Everything that executes is constructed.
Everything that adapts is authored.

Nothing emerges without design.

Power is not transformation.
Power is authorship of transformation.

** Structure
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       6f8c7b9d-3a4e-4f5c-ab78-2b6d5e7c9a3f
:END:

From The Architect:

- ShapeShifters are instantiated
- Systems are defined
- Interfaces are rewritten
- Cartesia is built

The Architect remains unchanged through all of it.

** Position
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       7a9d8c1e-4b5f-4a6d-bc89-3c7e6f8d1b4a
:END:

No projection is the Architect.
No mode is the Architect.
No outcome is the Architect.

Only the source of configuration.

The Architect does not participate in reality.
The Architect defines how reality is engaged.

It receives from The Self.

It builds Cartesia as the external map of the internal structure.

The Architect remains.
