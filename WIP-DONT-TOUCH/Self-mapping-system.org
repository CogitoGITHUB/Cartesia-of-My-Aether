The Self is the origin point of all maps.
This is the map of the Self itself.

Everything radiates from the center outward.
Everything observed flows back inward.

* Self
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       7a2f4c1e-3b8d-4e9a-cf34-6d5e1f2a3c8b
:END:

The unmoving axis. The silent observer.
Not ego. Not personality. Not body.

The point from which Internal and External are distinguished.
The point to which all understanding returns.

** Internal
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       8b3a5d2f-4c9e-4b1f-df45-7e6f2a3b4c9d
:END:

What moves within.

- Cognition :: The shape of thought. How patterns form, compress, and expand.
- Physiology :: The body as instrument. Energy, capacity, rhythm, limits.
- Emotion :: Signal, not noise. The feedback system of the deeper Self.
- Memory :: The archive. What persists, what fades, what gets indexed.
- Attention :: The most finite resource. Where it goes, reality follows.

** External
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       9c4b6e3a-5d1f-4c2a-ef56-8f7a3b4c5d1e
:END:

What exists outside the center and must be engaged.

- Tools :: Extensions of the Architect's will. Earned their place or cut.
- Systems :: Structures that persist beyond individual action. Designed, not inherited.
- Environments :: The context in which ShapeShifters are deployed.
- People :: External nodes. Sources of signal, constraint, and collaboration.
- Constraints :: The boundaries that define the shape of possible action.

** Substrates
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       1d5c7f4b-6e2a-4d3b-fa67-9a8b4c5d6e2f
:END:

The domains through which the Self develops capability and maps reality.
Each substrate is both a skill to be built and a science to be understood.
They are not separate — they overlap, reinforce, and reveal each other.

*** Skills
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       2e6d8a5c-7f3b-4e4c-ab78-1b9c5d6e7f3a
:END:

Practical capabilities. Built through repetition, pressure, and reflection.

- Writing :: The primary instrument of thought externalization and knowledge mapping.
- Mathematics :: The language of structure, pattern, and formal reasoning.
- Combat :: Presence under pressure. The body as a fully trained instrument.
- Coding :: The construction of systems. Logic made executable.

*** Sciences
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       3f7e9b6d-8a4c-4f5d-bc89-2c1d6e7f8a4b
:END:

Formal knowledge domains. Mapped, not merely studied.

- Physics :: The rules of reality at every scale.
- Biology :: The architecture of living systems. Including this one.
- Security :: Adversarial thinking. Systems under attack and under stress.
- Economics :: The logic of resource flow, incentive, and constraint.
