#+title:      Insert Position Prompt
#+priority:   1

* Insert Position (headings only)
Why: Control where the new heading goes among its siblings. Last is the
(default) — append at the end. First puts it right under the parent heading.

** Default (last or first)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-position ()
  "Prompt for heading insert position.  Returns nil (first) or 'last."
  (let ((p (completing-read "Insert position (last/first): "
                            '("last" "first") nil t "last")))
    (if (string= p "first") nil 'last)))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :name 'position
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-position
  :contexts '(heading)
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx) `(:after ,val)))
#+end_src
