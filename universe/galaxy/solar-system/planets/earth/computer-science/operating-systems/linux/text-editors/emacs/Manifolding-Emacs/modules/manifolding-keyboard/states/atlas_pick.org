#+title: Manifolding Keyboard — Atlas-Pick State
#+priority: 20

Whole block, all external prompt functions.
** State definition
Self-registration: mode and keymap in one unit (manual activation only). Delete this file and the state unregisters on next boot.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(manifolding-keyboard-define-state "atlas-pick"
  :sparse t
  :lighter "[PCK]"
  :cursor-type 'box)
#+end_src
** Atlas Pick
Flow: session pickers open with an auto-armed key-selection loop
(org-prompts.org) that owns letters for jumps+marking and takes
SPC/RET/C-q/C-l/C-e. This
keymap mirrors the control keys so they work there and in standalone
pickers too; the letter binds below apply only when the loop is not
armed.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(manifolding-keyboard-define-keys
  :states '("atlas-pick")
  :bind
  `(;; Selection
    ("RET"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-accept)
    ("<return>" . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-accept)
    ("C-c C-c" . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-accept)
    ("a"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--ks-to-option)
    ("m"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-mark)
    ("C-l"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--make-unfolding)
    ("C-e"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--edit-unfolding-file)
    ;; Session navigation
    ("SPC"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--session-back)
    ("b"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--session-back)
    ("B"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--session-revisit)
    ;; Cancellation
    ("q"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-abort)
    ("C-q"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-abort)
    ("C-c C-k" . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--buffer-abort)
    ("Q"       . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--quit-all)
    ("C-g"     . my/manifolding-atlas-org-prompt--quit-all)
    ;; Org outline navigation
    ("h"       . org-previous-visible-heading)
    ("H"       . org-backward-heading-same-level)
    ("t"       . org-next-visible-heading)
    ("T"       . org-forward-heading-same-level)
    ("n"       . org-cycle)))
#+end_src
