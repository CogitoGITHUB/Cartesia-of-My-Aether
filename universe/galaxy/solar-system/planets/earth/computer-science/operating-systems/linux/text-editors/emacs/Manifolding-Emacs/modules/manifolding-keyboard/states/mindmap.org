#+title: Manifolding Keyboard — Mindmap State
#+priority: 20

Keys specific to the mindmap state. Shared bindings live in universal.org.

** State definition
Self-registration: mode and keymap in one unit (manual activation only). Delete this file and the state unregisters on next boot.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(manifolding-keyboard-define-state "mindmap"
  :lighter "[MAP]"
  :cursor-type 'box)
#+end_src
** Bindings
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(manifolding-keyboard-define-keys
  :states '("mindmap")
  :bind
  `(
    ("A" . mm/assign-after)
    ("B" . my/manifolding-atlas-mastering-review-subtree)
    ("ESC" . my/mindmap-traverse-quit)
    ("P" . mm/assign-parent)
    ("g" . mm/group-display)
    ("h" . mm/goto-prev-sibling)
    ("i" . my/insert-state)
    ("m" . mm/map-display)
    ("n" . mm/traverse-child)
    ("q" . my/mindmap-traverse-quit)
    ("r" . mm/goto-root)
    ("s" . mm/goto-parent)
    ("t" . mm/goto-next-sibling)
))
#+end_src
