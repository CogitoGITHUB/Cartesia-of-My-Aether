#+title:      TODO State Prompt
#+priority:   1

* TODO_STATE :todo:file:heading:task:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: todo
:TASK-DEFAULT: TODO
:END:
** TODO
Not started yet, no other qualifier applies.
** NEXT
The immediate next actionable item.
** DOING
Actively being worked on right now.
** TESTING
Implementation done, currently verifying it works.
** REVIEW
Done but awaiting review or check before closing.
** BLOCKED
Stuck on something external — a dependency, resource, or other task.
** STUCK
Stuck on your own approach or understanding, not an external blocker.
** WAITING
Expecting a reply, response, or event before continuing.
** DEFERRED
Pushed to later deliberately, still committed to doing it.
** SOMEDAY
Maybe eventually, no commitment.
** BACKLOG
Queued and committed, just not prioritized yet.
** APPROVED
Approved, ready to proceed or already proceeding.
** REJECTED
Rejected, will not proceed as originally proposed.
** NEEDS-REVISION
Reviewed and sent back for changes before it can proceed.
** SCHEDULED
Has a fixed future date/time to happen.
** RECURRING
Repeats on a regular cadence.
** DRAFT
Early idea stage, before it's a committed task.
** DELEGATED
Handed off to someone else to do.
** FAILED
Attempted but did not succeed.
** ABANDONED
Started, then deliberately given up on.
** ARCHIVED
No longer active, kept for reference only.
** SUPERSEDED
Replaced by a newer task/decision.
** DONE
Completed successfully.
** CANCELLED
Decided not to do, before or without attempting.
