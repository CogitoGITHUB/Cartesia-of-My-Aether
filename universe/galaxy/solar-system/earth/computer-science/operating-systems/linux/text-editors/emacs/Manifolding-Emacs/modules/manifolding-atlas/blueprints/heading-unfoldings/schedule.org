#+title:      Scheduled / Deadline Prompts
#+priority:   1



* Scheduled / Deadline
Why: Plan when to start or finish a note. ~SCHEDULED~ appears in the agenda
from that date onward; ~DEADLINE~ appears as due.  Enter org timestamps like
~<2025-06-01 Sun>~ or leave blank for none.

** Scheduled Prompt
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-scheduled ()
  "Prompt for a SCHEDULED timestamp.  Returns nil or a string."
  (let ((raw (read-string "SCHEDULED (e.g. <2025-06-01 Sun> or blank): ")))
    (unless (string-empty-p raw) raw)))
#+end_src

** Registration

*** Scheduled registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'scheduled
  :name 'scheduled
  :label "Scheduled"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-scheduled
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "SCHEDULED"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val ctx)
              (if val
                  (if (eq ctx 'heading)
                      `(:post-apply (,(apply-partially #'my/manifolding-atlas--heading-schedule-apply val)))
                    `(:unfoldings (("SCHEDULED" . ,val))))
                `(:unfoldings (("SCHEDULED" . "WARNING"))))))
#+end_src
