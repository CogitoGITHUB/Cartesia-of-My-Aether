#+title:      Extra Unfoldings Prompt
#+priority:   1

* Extra Unfoldings
Why: Store arbitrary key-value metadata on notes. Used by manifolding-atlas queries,
org queries, and custom workflows. Press blank key to stop adding more.
Unfoldings become queryable in the manifolding-atlas DB.

** Default (loop until blank key)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-unfoldings ()
  "Prompt for extra unfolding key-value pairs.
Returns an alist of (key . value).  Press blank key to finish."
  (let (unfoldings)
    (catch 'done
      (while t
        (let ((k (read-string "Unfolding key (blank to stop): ")))
          (if (string-empty-p k)
              (throw 'done unfoldings)
            (push (cons k (read-string (format "Value for %s: " k)))
                  unfoldings)))))
    (nreverse unfoldings)))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'unfoldings
  :name 'unfoldings
  :label "Extra Unfoldings"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-unfoldings
  :contexts '(file heading)
  :read-current-fn nil
  :merge-strategy 'merge
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (when val `(:unfoldings ,val))))
#+end_src
