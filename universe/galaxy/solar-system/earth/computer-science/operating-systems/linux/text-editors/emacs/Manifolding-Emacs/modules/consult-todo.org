* TODO Consult Todo

** Purpose
Fast, no-frills search across TODO/FIXME/BUG/HACK keywords in buffers
and projects using consult. No views, no query language — just a live
picker that lands you on the line.

** Setup

*** Guix
#+begin_src scheme
emacs-consult-todo  ; custom declaration in config.scm
#+end_src

** Customization

*** Config
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(setq consult-todo-narrow
      '((?t . "TODO")
        (?f . "FIXME")
        (?b . "BUG")
        (?h . "HACK")
        (?n . "NOTE")))
#+end_src
