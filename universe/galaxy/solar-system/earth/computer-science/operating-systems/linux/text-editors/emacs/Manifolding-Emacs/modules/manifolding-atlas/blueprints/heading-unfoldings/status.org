#+title:      Status Prompt
#+priority:   5

Workflow state for a note's participation in active practice. This is
deliberately a separate blueprint from STAGE: lifecycle states are not
mastery states. You can suspend a beginner subject, an expert subject,
an unfinished research question, or a dormant skill — the stage
attainment survives underneath, and resuming restores exactly where
the knowledge left off. Stage says how far the knowledge developed;
status says what the mapper is currently doing with it.

* STATUS :inline:file:heading:task:
** ACTIVE
In the active practice pool: due for review on schedule, visible in
review sessions, counted in due tallies. The default for every living
note.
** SUSPENDED
Frozen on purpose. The schedule row is kept (nothing is deleted), the
stage value is untouched, but the note leaves due queries and review
sessions until resumed. Use for: "not now" topics, seasonal skills,
paused projects, anything whose absence from review is a decision
rather than neglect. Resume with the same toggle.
** ARCHIVED
Finished business kept for reference: completed projects, superseded
notes, closed questions. Out of review permanently unless deliberately
reactivated. Unlike SUSPENDED, archival asserts the note needs no
future practice — only its material.
** DORMANT
Neither practiced nor closed: hibernating knowledge that may or may
not wake. The difference from SUSPENDED is intent — dormant means "no
decision yet" while suspended means "decided pause". Dormant notes are
excluded from review but included in periodic triage counts, so they
resurface for a decision instead of rotting silently.
